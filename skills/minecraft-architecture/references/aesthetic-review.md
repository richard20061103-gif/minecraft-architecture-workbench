# Architectural and aesthetic review

Translate references into relationships: proportion, material hierarchy and movement through space. Define the first focal point, secondary rhythm and quiet area. Review the site silhouette, approach, human-height room, joinery/detail and night separately.

Reject unsupported eaves, floating roof corners, arbitrary ornament, featureless box walls, blocked windows, bare unfinished backs, exposed repeated lamps and furniture with no use. Trace every eave tip to a beam, bracket, post or wall; every stair to an opening, landing, rail and destination. Make roof and floor thickness credible at Minecraft scale.

Give each occupied floor a distinct function and a coherent floor, ceiling and light system. Preserve breathing room. Grade slopes in planted terraces or gentle transitions rather than sheer retaining walls unless the site calls for one. At water edges, combine bank profile, depth, stones, planting and reflected lighting rather than a hard rectangular rim.

For East Asian references, vary eave courses with slabs, stairs, tile rolls, corner beams and bracket density while keeping them connected. For modern work, use fewer stronger planes, careful joints and recesses. Style mixing requires a dominant language. Vanilla block light itself is colorless; warm appearance comes from fixture materials and shaders.

Before release or construction, score each category 0–3: silhouette, approach, terrain fit, structural joints, circulation, interior use, material control, night composition. A score below 2 identifies a concrete correction, not an unbounded redesign. Record the fix and make at most one focused pass.
