# Bounded survey and provenance

Record world ID, edition/version, dimension, bounds, source (live bridge, offline save or user image), source timestamp and capture method. An image can show character but cannot establish hidden terrain. Offline save data may lag a running game. Do not treat it as live state before writing.

For a new site, scan a coarse elevation grid, protected structures and main sightlines. Expand only where footprint, access or grading depends on the result. Mark unknown cells. For an existing build, read structural contact planes, selected sections, openings and adjoining circulation. Do not excavate or flatten merely to simplify the model.

Cache surveys with a dependency list. Later edits invalidate overlapping bounds and contact edges, not unrelated surveys. Before live placement, reread the exact diff area and protected contacts. Stop surveying once the design decisions can be made; write a compact record of constraints, terrain range, anchor, views, hazards and confidence.
