# Civic hall: monumental form with useful floors

This is a newly written generic case inspired by the process of adapting a large civic building to Minecraft. It does not reproduce an architectural plan, copyrighted drawing or real-world building coordinates.

[Newly drawn section diagram](diagram.svg) · [Local-coordinate planning data](layout.json). The numbers are illustrative and are not coordinates from a save.

**Failure observed:** the monumental shell looked convincing from afar but several floors were empty and dark; an oversized approach consumed the site's depth.

**Decision:** fix the approach-to-building ratio before adding detail. Give each level a civic purpose, clear partitions and distinct floor and light rhythm. Use a central atrium sculpture as an orientation point, then provide stair rails, visible landings, warm window interiors and a coherent wall wash.

**Gate:** compare every occupied floor separately. A large bright atrium cannot hide a dark gallery. Use a full walkable-cell light census where practical; report mean, minimum and fifth percentile by zone. Existing project-specific light bands are acceptance criteria for that project, not universal standards.
