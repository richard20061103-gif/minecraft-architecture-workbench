# Garden: terrain, water and restraint

This anonymized case captures reusable design decisions. It contains no save data, personal site bounds, raw images or third-party reference frames.

[Newly drawn plan diagram](diagram.svg) · [Local-coordinate planning data](layout.json). The numbers are illustrative and are not coordinates from a save.

**Failure observed:** perfectly rectangular courts, sheer exterior walls, abrupt pond rims, boxed rooms and scattered visible lamps gave a polished plan a rough lived experience.

**Decision:** reserve a central quiet plane, vary route width and reveal the pond gradually. Use planted terraces where the site falls. Step banks through stone, soil and vegetation. Put distinct functions and ceiling/light systems in occupied rooms. Make concealed fixtures physically able to illuminate the walking surface.

**Gate:** assess the approach, human-height view and night view separately. Confirm every path reaches its destination; in a connected world, measure actual block light at a declared walkability mask and inspect dark corners.
