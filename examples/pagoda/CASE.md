# Pagoda: a fifth tier that belongs to the tower

This is an anonymized decision study, not a copied world, tutorial model or build recipe. It contains no original coordinates or screenshots.

[Newly drawn section diagram](diagram.svg) · [Local-coordinate planning data](layout.json). The numbers are illustrative and are not coordinates from a save.

**Failure observed:** a generic cap roof and detached corner blocks made the top look like another building. A straight stair also consumed too much of the small room.

**Decision:** measure an existing eave course and repeat its tile, slab, bracket and beam rhythm at a slightly inset size. Lower the final room rather than compressing the lower tiers. Give the top one clear use: quiet viewing and display. Put a short turning flight against the wall, with a landing and verified headroom.

**Gate:** trace all lifted corners to posts, inspect the eave underside and stair from player height, compare target blocks to the live before-state, then read back the construction area. A screenshot alone does not prove support or walkability.
